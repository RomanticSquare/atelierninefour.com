About

Built from the inside of how leadership teams actually run.

JF

Jenny François · Founder

For nearly twelve years at a Fortune Global 500 consumer technology company, I ran day-to-day operations for four VPs and their teams, about 90 people across national sales, product marketing and retail account teams. The title said executive support. The work was operations: building the rhythm leaders decided in, turning loose priorities into owned plans, and keeping cross-functional work moving between teams that didn't report to each other.

I also led people. I mentored and coordinated the division's executive assistants from 2018, onboarding more than a dozen new hires a year. I directed a two-week sales academy for cohorts of 30+ new hires, partnering with HR, Finance, Legal and Compliance. And I rose to National Vice Chair of an employee resource group, leading executive-facing programs for 150 to 180+ attendees.

I completed a Master of Arts in Organizational Leadership in 2026, with every course at Exceeds Proficiency. I build my own decision-support tools with AI every day, so I know where it helps and where it gets in the way.

How I work

Three principles that don't bend.

Experience over theory

Every recommendation comes from having run the work, not from a framework borrowed from a much larger company.

Systems thinking

A recurring problem is a design flaw, not a people flaw. Fix the system and the people get their time back.

Honest claims

Clear scope, defensible advice and no inflated promises. If something isn't worth automating or building, I'll say so.

The name

Atelier Nine Four

Atelier is French for a working studio: a place where craft is practiced seriously, over time. Nine Four is a personal signature. Together they describe how I approach operations: as a craft, built carefully for each team, by a specific person who stands behind the work.
