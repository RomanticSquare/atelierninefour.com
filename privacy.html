Privacy notice

Effective October 2026.

What this site collects. This site uses Google Analytics to understand which pages are visited and how people find them. Google Analytics uses cookies and collects information such as pages viewed, approximate region and device type. It does not give me your name or email address.

Inquiries. The contact form is processed by Formspree, a form service that delivers what you submit (your name, work email, message and any optional details) to my email inbox. I use it only to reply to you and to discuss possible work. Formspree's handling of that data is covered by its privacy policy. If you write to me by email instead, it arrives as a normal email.

What I don't do. I don't sell or rent personal information, and I don't add you to a mailing list without your permission.

Your choices. You can block analytics cookies in your browser settings or with Google's opt-out add-on. To ask what information I hold from your inquiry or emails, or to have it deleted, write to jenny@atelierninefour.com.

Changes. If this notice changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new effective date.
