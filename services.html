Lead offer

Operational Clarity Diagnostic

2 weeksFixed fee

For teams that know something is off but can't name exactly what. I map how decisions, meetings and reporting really work today and tell you plainly what to fix first.

Included

  • Intake questionnaire
  • Two stakeholder interviews
  • One end-to-end process map
  • Findings memo with your top five fixes
  • 45-minute readout with your leadership

Not included

  • Implementing the fixes (that's the Build)
  • Access to confidential client or employee data
  • Additional interviews beyond scope, unless added by change order

How it runs

  • The two weeks start once the scope is signed, the deposit is paid and your intake is back.
  • You choose the one process to map at kickoff, and we agree how success will be judged.
  • Your team's time: about an hour for intake, two 60-minute interviews with people you choose, and the 45-minute readout.

Investment

  • A fixed fee set by team size and the number of stakeholders involved.
  • Quoted in writing within two business days of the fit call.
  • The full Diagnostic fee is credited toward an Operating System Build when the Build scope is signed within 30 days of the findings readout.

AI Adoption Sprint

3 to 4 weeksFixed scope

Typically includes

  • Readiness check across teams and workflows
  • Tool selection criteria and use cases worth piloting

Guardrails

How engagements work

Clear terms protect your budget and my time.

Written scope

Every engagement has a one-page scope: what's in, what's out, the number of sessions and rounds of revision, and the fee.

Change orders, not scope creep

Good ideas come up mid-project. They get a short add-on scope with its own price and timeline, and you decide.

Simple payment terms

A deposit to start, the balance on delivery. Final deliverables are released once the invoice is paid.

Questions

Before you reach out

What size teams do you work with?

Mostly 10 to 100 people: professional services firms, nonprofits, and creative or marketing agencies where the founder or COO is still holding too much of the operation in their head.

How is pricing set?

Each engagement is a fixed fee, quoted in writing after the fit call once the scope is clear. You won't be billed by the hour or surprised by an invoice.

Do you guarantee results?

No one honestly can. What I commit to is a clear scope, the deliverables in it, and measures we agree on up front so you can judge whether the work is paying off.

Do you need access to confidential information?

Rarely, and never through email or a web form. If a project needs sensitive data, we agree on how it's shared and handled before any work starts.

Do you work remotely?

Yes. Sessions run by video call, and deliverables are shared in tools your team already uses.
